Opening PHILSYNTH on macOS
=========================

PHILSYNTH is not notarized by Apple, so macOS may block it the first time.
Only proceed if you trust the source and know the app has not been altered.

1. Double-click PHILSYNTH.app. If macOS blocks it, dismiss the warning.
2. Open the Apple menu and choose System Settings.
3. Select Privacy & Security, then scroll down to the Security section.
4. Next to the message about PHILSYNTH, click Open Anyway.
5. If asked, authenticate with your Mac administrator password or Touch ID.
6. When the warning appears again, click Open.

You can normally open PHILSYNTH by double-clicking it after this. If Open
Anyway is not shown, try opening PHILSYNTH again, then return to Privacy &
Security. On a managed Mac, your administrator may restrict this option.

If macOS says the app is damaged or contains malware, do not bypass the
warning. Obtain a fresh copy from a trusted source instead.

Apple's guidance: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
