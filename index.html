Audio export level

Choose how to set the exported audio level. Neither option changes your project or live playback.

Original Dynamics: Keep the mix's original output level. Peaks above 0 dBFS will be clipped in the exported file.

Peak Normalization: Raise or lower the entire mix so its highest sample peak is -1 dBFS. The same gain is applied to both channels, preserving quiet/loud relationships and stereo balance without compression. This does not make all projects equally loud or repair distortion already in the mix; compressed formats may introduce new peaks.
